BEFORE YOU START

Good to know.

Will the parts fit my exact variant?

Studio collects your variant and flags upgrades that need inspection. Exact part compatibility needs to be confirmed before any installation.

Can I choose upgrades without cutting factory wiring?

Use the no-factory-wiring-cuts filter in Studio. Ask your manufacturer or authorised dealer about warranty coverage for the exact accessory and installation method.

Can I book a real installation today?

The current Studio is a public demo. Estimates are illustrative, and requests are saved in your browser. Live workshop scheduling and payments are planned.
